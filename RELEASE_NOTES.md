## What's new in 1.1.2

Version 1.1.2 improves regional language handling, symbol routing, number formatting, and updates compatibility:

- **Punjabi script differentiation**: Differentiated Gurmukhi (Indian) Punjabi and Shahmukhi (Pakistani) Punjabi with proper native names in settings and profile selectors.
- **Typing and deletion symbol routing**: Fixed language switching for language-specific symbols (such as Urdu khatma `۔`, Arabic comma `،`, and question mark `؟`) during typing and deletion, routing them to the correct language synthesizer.
- **Dedicated Date and Other Numbers reading options**: Refined mobile phone pattern matching to prevent dates or general numbers from being misidentified as phone numbers. Added dedicated configurable formats for dates (with month names, day-month-year, etc.) and other numbers (default synthesizer reading, individual spaced digits, or grouped).
- **NVDA 2026.3.1 compatibility**: Tested and verified fully compatible with NVDA 2026.3.1.
